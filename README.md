# Push x Bite: the AI policy navigator

A single-page app that compares AI policy across 16 jurisdictions using the push and bite framework.

- **Push** is the pressure on a government to act. Eight factors are rated 0 to 3: four on the promotion side (rivalry, sovereignty, growth, industry) and four on the protection side (rights, harm, labour, control).
- **Bite** is what the policy does. Constraining bite has six components and enabling bite has four.
- Each has a direction and a strength, which gives two linked 3 x 3 charts.

Scores are judgements made from the research in this repo. They are not measurements, and they cover government intent only. The reference date is 3 October 2026, and the Australia content was updated on 9 October 2026.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app (HTML, CSS, JavaScript and SVG, no build step). Open it in a browser.
- `research/report.md`: the research report the scores are based on.
- `research/notes/`: the working notes behind the report, one file per region.

## Australia update, 9 October 2026

The research notes predate this update. It added the 15 July 2026 announcement of mandatory national AI standards, the 18 September 2026 consultation paper (closed 9 October), the September 2026 Digital Duty of Care exposure draft, and the October 2026 parliamentary hearings, using ABC News, SBS and law firm summaries.
